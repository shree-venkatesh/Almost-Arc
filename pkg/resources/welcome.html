Almost Arc makes Zen Browser look and feel like Arc.

This installer:

  • installs Zen from Zen's official releases, if you don't have it yet,
  • installs Almost Arc into Zen,
  • adds the almost-arc command for updates later.

If Zen is open, the installer asks to quit it.

Almost Arc is an independent, free mod. Zen is made by the Zen team, and isn't changed apart from adding the mod. Not affiliated with Zen, The Browser Company or Arc.
